# Your ADHD Entrepreneurial Profile

Now that you understand the ADHD superpowers, it's time to reflect on your own cognitive style and how it applies to your entrepreneurial journey.

## Reflection Questions

Take a few minutes to think deeply about these questions. You might want to write down your answers.

### 1. Recognizing Your Superpowers

**Think about a time when you entered hyperfocus mode.**
- What were you working on?
- How long did you work without distraction?
- What made that task engaging enough to capture your hyperfocus?
- How can you design more of your startup work to trigger this state?

### 2. Your Pattern Recognition

**When have you made unexpected connections between unrelated ideas?**
- What different domains or experiences did you connect?
- Did this lead to a creative solution or insight?
- How can you intentionally expose yourself to diverse inputs to fuel this strength?

### 3. Crisis Performance

**Recall a high-pressure situation where you performed well.**
- What was the situation?
- How did you respond differently than usual?
- Why do you think pressure helped rather than hurt your performance?
- How might you use time-boxing or deadlines to create productive urgency?

### 4. Your Interest-Based Nervous System

**List three business tasks you find genuinely interesting:**
1. _________________
2. _________________
3. _________________

**List three business tasks you find painfully boring:**
1. _________________
2. _________________
3. _________________

**Now ask yourself:**
- Can I delegate, automate, or eliminate the boring tasks?
- How can I restructure my startup so I spend 80% of time on interesting work?
- What would my business look like if I designed it entirely around my interest-based nervous system?

### 5. The Authenticity Question

**Have you been trying to build a "normal" startup using "normal" methods?**
- Where have you been fighting your ADHD instead of leveraging it?
- What entrepreneurial advice have you received that feels impossible to follow?
- What would change if you gave yourself permission to build differently?

## Reframing Your Narrative

Many ADHD founders spend years believing they're "broken entrepreneurs" who need to be fixed. That's backwards.

**Old Story:**
"I struggle with entrepreneurship because I have ADHD. If I could just be more disciplined, organized, and focused like successful founders, I'd make it work."

**New Story:**
"I have unique cognitive superpowers that give me advantages in creativity, crisis management, and rapid execution. My job is to build a startup that leverages these strengths and systematically addresses my challenges—not to become someone I'm not."
