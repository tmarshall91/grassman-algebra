# "I Thought This Would Take 20 Minutes"

**10:00 AM:** "I'll quickly fix this bug before my 10:30 meeting. Should take 20 minutes max."

**11:45 AM:** You look up from your screen. You've missed the meeting. You've been coding for almost 2 hours. How did that happen?

Or the opposite:

**Monday:** "I'll ship this feature by Friday. Plenty of time—it's only 5 days away."

**Friday, 5 PM:** You've barely started. Where did the week go?

## Welcome to Time Blindness

**Time blindness** is the ADHD tendency to lose track of time, struggle to perceive how much time has passed, and consistently underestimate how long tasks will take.

It's not poor planning. It's not laziness. **Your brain literally perceives time differently.**

## Why This Matters for Founders

Entrepreneurship runs on deadlines:
- Launch dates
- Investor meetings
- Customer commitments
- Payroll deadlines
- Conference submissions

When you can't accurately perceive time, every deadline becomes a crisis. Every estimate becomes a broken promise. Every schedule becomes a source of shame.

## The Pattern

You're probably familiar with these:
- "This will take an hour" → Takes 6 hours
- "I have all day" → Day ends, nothing done
- "The deadline is next week" → Feels infinitely far away until it's tomorrow
- Hyperfocus for 4 hours, lose track of everything

**Traditional time management advice assumes you can perceive time accurately. You can't.**

But you can build external time awareness systems that work anyway.

Let's learn how.
