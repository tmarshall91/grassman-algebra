## Part 3: The System Design Checklist

For the external system you're most excited to implement, verify it meets these criteria:

- [ ] **Captures immediately** - Doesn't rely on memory or "doing it later"
- [ ] **Requires zero/minimal maintenance** - Works even when you have no executive function
- [ ] **Removes decisions** - Pre-commits or automates choices
- [ ] **Designed for low-energy states** - Works when you're exhausted or unfocused
- [ ] **Actually excites you** - You want to use it (leverages interest-based nervous system)

If your system checks most boxes, it's likely to stick.

## Part 4: The Self-Compassion Reframe

Many ADHD founders have internalized negative messages about their executive function challenges:

**Identify your inner critic:**
- "I should be able to just remember things without writing them down"
- "Normal founders don't need all these systems"
- "If I was more disciplined, I wouldn't need external accountability"
- "I'm too disorganized to run a company"

**Now reframe each statement:**

| Inner Critic | Reframe |
|--------------|---------|
| "I should just remember things" | "My brain works differently. Using external capture is smart, not weak." |
| "Normal founders don't need systems" | "All successful founders use systems. Mine are just more explicit because I can't rely on invisible mental systems." |
| "I need more discipline" | "I need better systems. Discipline is a neurotypical solution to a neurotypical problem." |
| "I'm too disorganized" | "Building external organization systems is a learnable skill, and it will make my startup more scalable." |

## Part 5: The Hidden Advantage

Answer this question honestly:

**"How have my executive function challenges forced me to build better systems than I might have otherwise?"**

Examples to consider:
- Do you document more because you can't remember?
- Have you automated things others do manually?
- Have you created clearer processes because you can't hold complex procedures in your head?
- Have you delegated or hired earlier than bootstrapped founders typically do?

**Your executive function challenges might be secretly building a more systematic, scalable business.**

## The Acceptance Principle

Repeat this to yourself:

**"My executive functions work differently. That's not a moral failing or a character flaw. My job isn't to become neurotypical—it's to build systems that work with my brain as it actually is."**

Successful ADHD founders don't waste energy wishing they were different. They invest that energy in systems that compensate for their challenges and amplify their strengths.
