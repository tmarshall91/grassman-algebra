# Reflecting on Vibe Coding

Take a moment to think about what you've learned and how it applies to your development practice.

## Reflection Prompts

Consider these questions. There are no right or wrong answers—this is about connecting the concepts to your experience.

### 1. Your Current Coding Experience

Think about your last few coding sessions:
- When did you feel most "in flow"? What enabled that state?
- When did you get interrupted or stuck? What caused those disruptions?
- How much time did you spend on creative problem-solving vs. mechanical implementation?

**Reflection**: Which of those interruptions or mechanical tasks could vibe coding help with?

### 2. Mindset Shifts

The move to vibe coding requires thinking differently:
- From "writing code" to "designing systems"
- From "memorizing syntax" to "expressing intent"
- From "lone developer" to "AI pair programmer"

**Reflection**: Which of these shifts feels most natural to you? Which feels most challenging?

### 3. Your Learning Style

Different developers have different relationships with learning:
- Some prefer deep dives into documentation
- Others learn by doing and experimenting
- Some like structured tutorials
- Others learn through trial and error

**Reflection**: How might vibe coding change your learning approach? Could it accelerate learning in areas where you typically struggle?

### 4. Concerns and Curiosity

It's natural to have both excitement and concerns about a new approach.

**Reflection**:
- What excites you most about vibe coding?
- What concerns or doubts do you have?
- What would you want to try first in a real project?

## Looking Forward

As you continue learning vibe coding, keep these questions in mind:
- Am I using AI to think for me, or to amplify my thinking?
- Am I understanding the code being generated?
- Am I maintaining creative control over the architecture?
- Am I enjoying the process more?

The goal isn't to answer these perfectly every time—it's to stay intentional about how you're collaborating with AI.

## Personal Action

Complete this sentence:

**"In my next coding session, I will try vibe coding for..."**

(Examples: writing tests, refactoring a messy function, learning a new library, building a prototype feature)

Choose something low-stakes where you can experiment without pressure.

---

Remember: Vibe coding is a practice, not a prescription. You're learning to collaborate with AI in a way that works for **your** style and **your** goals.
